    Abilities

    Has the ability to change his size, as well the size of objects of his choosing that he is in contact with. The process can consume a lot of energy (energy used grows exponentially as he gets bigger). Due to the energy used he prefers being his usual size, only using the ability when the situation calls for it. Archy has been seen as short as 2cm tall.

    Backstory

    After being mistaken for part of a toy set, Archy's egg was wrapped up as a Christmas gift. With Australia and Austria sounding far too alike at that time of night, Santa touched down at the wrong address - Fred waking on Christmas morning to a very unexpected delivery. It wasn't long before Archy hatched and was raised by Fred into the loveable creature he is today.

    How to figure out his weight

    Archy weighs 0.8585 grams per milliliter (85.85 miligrams per cubic millimetre)

    Equation to calculate weight (X being length in cm from nose to tail):

    Volume cubic cm = (0.171359X)^3

    Mass g = (0.171359X)^3 *0.8585
